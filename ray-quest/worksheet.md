## Goal
Use ray diagrams, the law of reflection, and Snell's law to predict where the beam will cross the fixed receiver line before testing in the game.

## Conventions

- Angles are measured from the `+x` axis.
- Positive rotation is counterclockwise.
- Grid coordinates use `+x` to the right and `+y` upward.
- In Snell's law, angles are measured from the normal.

Useful equations:

- Reflection: `theta_i = theta_r`
- Refraction: `n_1 sin(theta_1) = n_2 sin(theta_2)`
